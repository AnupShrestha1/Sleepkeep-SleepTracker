# Sleepkeep Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of publishing]_

Sleepkeep ("the app") is a sleep log for Android made by [developer name]. This policy explains
what the app stores and where. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is
sent to anyone.

## What the app stores

When you use Sleepkeep, it stores on your phone:

- **Sleep sessions:** when you tapped Start sleep and End sleep, and any changes you made to those
  times.
- **Morning check-in answers:** how long you think it took to fall asleep and how long you were
  awake. Both are optional.
- **Sound events,** only if you turn on sound monitoring: the time a loud sound started and
  ended, and how loud it was compared with the room's background noise.
- **Monitoring records:** when sound monitoring was running, and why it stopped.

## What the app does not do

- **No audio is saved.** When sound monitoring is on, the app listens through the microphone and
  analyses the sound on your phone as it happens. The audio itself is discarded immediately and
  is never stored, recorded or transmitted.
- **No data leaves your phone.** The app has no internet permission. It has no servers, accounts,
  cloud sync, ads, analytics or crash reporting. Nothing is shared with third parties.
- **No backup copies.** The app opts out of Android cloud backup and of device-to-device
  transfer, so its data isn't copied to Google Drive or to a new phone.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** used only during a sleep session for which you turned on sound monitoring, to
  notice loud sounds. The app asks before using it. You can say no and still use every other
  feature.
- **Notifications:** used to show the notification Android requires while monitoring runs.
- **Foreground service (microphone):** lets monitoring continue while the screen is off.

## Health information

Sleepkeep compares your sleep with general public guidelines for adults, from the US CDC and
NHLBI. It does not give medical advice, diagnose sleep disorders or measure sleep stages.

## Your control over your data

- Delete a single session on its night page.
- Delete everything under **Settings & privacy → Delete all data**.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.

Since no data is sent off your phone, the developer has no copy of it and can't access, export or
delete it for you.

## Children

Sleepkeep is intended for adults (18 and over). It doesn't knowingly handle data from children.
